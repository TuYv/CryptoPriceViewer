Crypto Price Viewer - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 (version 1.0.0)

English · 中文

Summary

Crypto Price Viewer ("the extension") does not collect, sell or share any personal information. It has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Everything it stores stays in your own browser.

What the extension stores

DataWhereWhy
Your settings: the coins you follow and their order, the pinned coin, currency, language, price colors, appearance, market page layout (tiles or list), refresh interval, toolbar icon mode and which news sources are onChrome sync storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs it to your other computers under your own Google account. The developer cannot see it.So your list looks the same on every computer you use
Your CoinGecko API key, if you add oneChrome local storage on this computer only; it is never syncedTo send it to CoinGecko with price requests
Cached prices, charts, news headlines, and the names and icon links of coins you addedChrome local storage on this computerTo show something right away and to avoid extra requests
A count of recent CoinGecko requestsChrome session storage; cleared when you close the browserTo stay under CoinGecko's rate limits

Removing the extension deletes all of this data from your browser.

Which websites the extension connects to

  • api.coingecko.com: to get prices, charts and search results. The request contains the coins you follow, the currency, and your API key if you added one. Coin icons are loaded from CoinGecko's image server.
  • News sites (www.panewslab.com, api.odaily.news, decrypt.co): only after you have opened the News page, and only for the sources you have turned on. PANews and Odaily let any web page read their public news feeds, so the extension reads them without asking; Decrypt is read only after you allow it. The extension downloads their public news feeds and shows headlines and links. You can turn off any source, or remove the permission, in Settings › News sources.

These sites handle requests under their own privacy policies. The extension does not send them any personal information.

Feedback and donations

The "Report a problem" link opens the project's GitHub Issues page in a new tab, and the donation page only shows QR codes. The extension itself does not send any data in either case. Anything you post on GitHub is covered by GitHub's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the settings and caches described above.
  • alarms: to refresh prices and news at the interval you choose.
  • https://api.coingecko.com/*: to request price data.
  • Optional: the three news sites above: Decrypt is requested when you choose to see its news. PANews and Odaily are requested only if they stop letting their news feeds be read directly.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and noted in the extension's release notes.

Contact

Please open an issue at https://github.com/TuYv/CryptoPriceViewer/issues.

概要

Crypto Price Viewer（下称“本扩展”）不收集、不出售、不分享任何个人信息，没有统计、跟踪和广告。它保存的所有数据都只在你自己的浏览器里。

本扩展保存了什么

数据保存在哪里用途
你的设置：关注的币和顺序、固定到工具栏的币、货币、语言、涨跌颜色、外观、行情页显示方式（色块或列表）、刷新间隔、工具栏图标显示方式、打开了哪些资讯来源Chrome 的同步存储。如果你登录了 Chrome 并打开了同步，Chrome 会在你自己的 Google 账号下把它同步到你的其他电脑。开发者看不到这些数据。让你在每台电脑上看到同样的列表
你填写的 CoinGecko 密钥（如果有）只保存在这台电脑的 Chrome 本地存储里，不会同步请求价格时一起发给 CoinGecko
价格、图表、新闻标题的缓存，以及你添加的币的名字和图标地址这台电脑的 Chrome 本地存储打开时马上有内容可看，并减少重复请求
最近向 CoinGecko 请求的次数Chrome 的会话存储，关闭浏览器后清空避免超过 CoinGecko 的请求次数限制

卸载本扩展后，以上数据会从你的浏览器里全部删除。

本扩展会访问哪些网站

  • api.coingecko.com：获取价格、图表和搜索结果。请求里包含你关注的币、货币，以及你填写的密钥（如果有）。币种图标从 CoinGecko 的图片服务器加载。
  • 新闻网站（www.panewslab.com、api.odaily.news、decrypt.co）：只有在你打开过资讯页之后，并且只访问你开着的来源。PANews 和 Odaily 允许任何网页读取它们公开的新闻，所以本扩展不用询问就能读；Decrypt 要你同意之后才读。本扩展下载它们公开的新闻订阅，只显示标题和链接。你可以在“设置 › 资讯来源”里关掉任意来源或收回权限。

这些网站按各自的隐私政策处理请求。本扩展不会向它们发送任何个人信息。

反馈和打赏

“反馈问题”只是在新标签页打开本项目的 GitHub Issues 页面，打赏页只显示二维码，本扩展在这两处都不发送任何数据。你在 GitHub 上发布的内容适用 GitHub 的隐私政策。

权限

  • storage：保存上面说的设置和缓存。
  • alarms：按你设置的间隔刷新价格和新闻。
  • https://api.coingecko.com/*：请求行情数据。
  • 可选：上面三个新闻网站：Decrypt 在你选择看它的新闻时申请；PANews 和 Odaily 只有在它们不再允许直接读取新闻时才申请。

儿童

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的信息。

变更

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在这个地址，并在扩展的更新说明里注明。
